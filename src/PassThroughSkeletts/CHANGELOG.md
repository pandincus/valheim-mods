# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-05

Initial release! With this mod, you can no-clip through your Skeletts.

- Players and summoned Skeletts pass through each other. No getting blocked or shoved!
- Skeletts stay solid to everything else (e.g. the ground, enemies) and can still be petted, renamed and hit
- Only one config setting: `Enabled`, to disable the mod functionality completely. Can toggle on and off instantly.
