# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-15

Initial release! Your magic barrier finally has a bar.

- A bar on your HUD showing how much barrier capacity you have left
- A bar under your allies' health bars when they have a barrier, including your Skeletts
- Recasting the barrier now refills it, instead of only resetting its timer
- Works in multiplayer (other players need the mod to share their bar with you, but the refill works for you either way)
- Configurable color, position and size, and a switch to turn the refill off
